## What is the Skydio X10?

The **Skydio X10** is an advanced autonomous drone designed for professional applications including:
- Public safety and emergency response
- Infrastructure inspection
- Thermal imaging and analysis
- Mapping and surveying
- Search and rescue
- Environmental monitoring

### Key Features

**Autonomy and Intelligence:**
- Advanced obstacle avoidance with 360° coverage
- AI-powered flight control
- Autonomous navigation capabilities
- Adaptive flight in complex environments

**Imaging Capabilities:**
- High-resolution visual camera
- Thermal imaging camera (FLIR)
- Multiple camera modes and settings
- Real-time video transmission

**Durability:**
- Weather-resistant design
- Cold weather operation capability
- Robust construction
- Protective propeller guards

**Flight Performance:**
- Extended flight time (up to 35-40 minutes depending on conditions)
- High wind resistance
- Precise positioning and control
- Multiple flight modes

## Thermal Imaging Capability

### FLIR Thermal Camera

The X10's thermal sensor is a key feature for many applications:

**Specifications:**
- **Thermal resolution:** 640 × 512 pixels
- **Thermal sensitivity:** <50 mK
- **Temperature range:** -20°C to +150°C (-4°F to +302°F)
- **Radiometric capability:** Measures actual temperatures

### Thermal Imaging Applications

**Building Inspections:**
- Heat loss detection
- Insulation deficiencies
- Moisture intrusion
- HVAC system performance
- Roof damage assessment

**Infrastructure:**
- Electrical system inspections
- Mechanical equipment monitoring
- Pipe leak detection
- Solar panel assessment

**Search and Rescue:**
- Locating persons in wilderness
- Disaster response
- Missing person searches
- Night operations

**Environmental:**
- Wildlife detection and monitoring
- Water temperature mapping
- Vegetation stress analysis

**Best Conditions for Thermal Imaging:**
- Temperature differential between target and environment
- Dawn, dusk, or nighttime for building inspections
- Calm weather conditions
- Clear skies (clouds/precipitation reduce effectiveness)

## Obstacle Avoidance System

### Skydio Autonomy

The X10's defining feature is its advanced obstacle avoidance:

**How It Works:**
- Six navigation cameras provide 360° awareness
- AI processes millions of data points per second
- Real-time 3D mapping of environment
- Predictive path planning

**Capabilities:**
- Navigate complex environments autonomously
- Avoid obstacles while tracking subjects
- Operate safely near structures
- Adapt to changing conditions

**Limitations:**
- Reduced performance in low light
- Glass and water can be challenging
- Very fine wires/cables may not be detected
- System should be trusted but not relied upon exclusively

**Configuration:**
- Adjustable sensitivity settings
- User can set obstacle avoidance parameters
- Different modes for different situations
- Balance between caution and agility

## Flight Modes

The X10 offers several flight modes:

### 1. Manual Skills Mode
- **Direct pilot control** using controller sticks
- **Best for:** Precision flying, learning fundamentals
- **Obstacle avoidance:** Active but allows closer approaches
- **When to use:** Training, precise positioning, manual control situations

### 2. GPS Mode (Positioning)
- **Position hold** using GPS
- **Automated hover** and stability
- **Best for:** Stable platform for imaging
- **When to use:** Thermal inspections, stationary observation

### 3. Autonomous Modes
- **Waypoint navigation**
- **Orbit/circle subjects**
- **Cable/power line inspection** modes
- **When to use:** Automated inspection workflows

## Battery Management

### Battery Best Practices

**Before Flight:**
- Charge batteries fully
- Check charge level (LED indicators)
- Ensure battery is firmly seated
- Verify battery contacts are clean

**During Operations:**
- Monitor battery level continuously
- Plan for 20% reserve minimum
- Cold weather reduces battery performance
- Wind increases power consumption

**After Flight:**
- Remove battery from aircraft
- Store at proper temperature
- Don't leave fully charged for extended periods
- Check for damage or swelling

**Cold Weather Considerations (Alaska):**
- Pre-warm batteries before flight
- Reduced flight time in cold temperatures
- Keep spare batteries warm
- Monitor voltage more closely
- Land with higher reserve (25-30%)

### Battery Indicators

**LED Status:**
- Green: 50-100% charge
- Yellow: 30-50% charge
- Red: Below 30% charge
- Flashing red: Critical low battery

## Safety Considerations

### General Safety

- **Always maintain visual line of sight**
- **Never fly over people** (unless specific permissions)
- **Respect privacy** and cultural sensitivities
- **Check weather conditions** before every flight
- **Know emergency procedures**
- **Have a spotter** when possible

### Alaska-Specific

- **Wildlife awareness** (birds, especially)
- **Rapidly changing weather**
- **Limited daylight** in winter
- **Cold affects battery performance**
- **Remote locations** - plan for emergencies
- **Cultural sites** - obtain proper permissions
